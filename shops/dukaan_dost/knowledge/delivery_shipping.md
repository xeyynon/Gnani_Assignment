## Delivery — डिलीवरी — డెలివరీ — home delivery
The shop delivers within 3 kilometres of the shop. Delivery is free for orders of 300 rupees or more and costs 20 rupees for smaller orders. Orders are usually delivered within 45 minutes during shop hours, 8 AM to 9:30 PM. Orders taken after 9 PM are delivered the next morning from 8 AM.

## Pick up from the shop — दुकान से लें
The caller can also collect the order from the shop, with no delivery charge. Ready in about 15 minutes.

## Outside the delivery area
If the caller is more than 3 kilometres away, say the shop cannot deliver there, and offer to keep the order for pick up.
