## Are the prices fixed — दाम — ధర — price
The prices are the shop's current prices. They can change, so the shopkeeper confirms the final bill on delivery.

## Can I change my order — बदलना — మార్చడం — change or cancel
The order can be changed or cancelled by phone until it leaves the shop. After that, tell the delivery person.

## Is there a discount — छूट — డిస్కౌంట్ — offer
The assistant does not offer discounts. If the caller asks, say the shopkeeper decides on discounts and will tell them.

## Fresh vegetables — सब्ज़ी — కూరగాయలు
Vegetables are sold by the kilo or by the packet shown in the list, and are bought fresh each morning.
