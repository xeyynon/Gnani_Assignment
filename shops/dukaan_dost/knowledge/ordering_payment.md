## How to pay — भुगतान — చెల్లింపు — payment options
The caller can pay in three ways: cash on delivery, UPI, or on credit (udhaar). Cash on delivery means paying the delivery person when the order arrives.

## UPI — यूपीआई
UPI payment can be made when the order arrives by scanning the delivery person's QR code, or in advance to the shop's UPI ID: dukaandost.demo@upi (a made-up ID for this demo). Never ask for a UPI PIN, card number or any password.

## Credit — उधार — అప్పు — udhaar
Regular customers can buy on credit (udhaar), up to a limit the shopkeeper sets for each customer. The assistant takes the caller's name and tells them the shopkeeper will confirm the credit. The assistant never promises credit and never states a credit limit.

## Minimum order — न्यूनतम ऑर्डर
The smallest order the shop delivers is 100 rupees.
