- Docs: https://docs.gnani.ai

## The challenge
Gnani.ai's **Great Indian AI Internship Challenge**: build a working voice agent on Gnani's models. Gnani lists a kirana phone-order agent as one of its own example ideas, which is what we're building.

## Eligibility
Enrolled at an Indian college or university, aged 18+, **solo or a team of two**. One entry each.

## Hard rules
- **Use at least one Gnani model, shown working in a public video.** We use two: Prisma v3.0 (STT) and Timbre v3.0 (TTS).
- **No misleading demos**, bought engagement, bots, paid promotion, engagement swaps or plagiarism.
- **Synthetic data only.** No real phone numbers, Aadhaar, PAN or recordings of real calls.
- **Deadline:** Nov 10, 2026, 11:59 PM IST.

## What gets you shortlisted
- **Genuine use of Gnani's technology** (eligibility check), then **verified engagement** (reactions, comments, reshares) on the nominated posts, **counted at the closing cutoff**.
- The post must be a **recorded video of the working app** on LinkedIn, X or both, tagged **#GnaniAI** and **#GreatIndianAIInternshipChallenge**, and **live before you submit**. Post early (by Nov 5).
- Then individual interviews. Internships (up to 100, six months from Jan 1, 2027, stipend up to ₹1 lakh a month) depend on skills, interview and fit.

## What to submit
- **Project details:** title, the problem, intended users, the Gnani models or APIs used, each member's contribution.
- **Final submission:** the demo video, a written explanation, the list of Gnani models or APIs, **one nominated post link per platform**.

Plus, from our own plan:
- A 60-second demo video
- A 2–3 minute walkthrough video

## Awards we're targeting
| Award | Why we qualify |
|---|---|
| Mixed Vibes | Code-mixed Hinglish and Telugu-English speech |
| Real Deal | A real kirana-shop problem |
| Noise Canceller | Simulated phone-line audio, evaluated with shop, traffic and fan noise at ~10 dB SNR |
| Solo Ninja | Built by one person |
| Main Character | Telugu as a first-class language |
| Demo Day Drop | The 60-second video |
| Jugaad Genius (stretch) | A whole phone-order agent that runs from one Python command and a browser tab |
| Crowd Favourite (stretch) | Public votes on the same posts |

There are ten awards in all (₹50,000 each, at most one per entry). Two-Person Army doesn't apply, and Small Town, Big Build depends on the college. See [external_research.md](external_research.md).
